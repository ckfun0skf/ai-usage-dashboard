STEP 1 · EXPORT FROM GROK

Export your usage events

  1. 1
    Open the Grok Usage dashboard

    Go to grok.com/dashboard/usage ↗ and sign in if prompted.

  2. 2
    Scroll to the Usage events table

    Scroll down the page until you see the table listing individual usage events.

  3. 3
    Click Export

    Use the Export control on the Usage events table to download the CSV file. Keep the downloaded CSV unchanged, including its header row.

  4. 4
    Import the downloaded CSV here

    Continue to Step 2 and choose the file. New events are added to your local history and duplicates are skipped.
